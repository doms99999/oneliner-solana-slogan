# One-Line Slogan for a Solana Wallet Agent

One slogan for a Solana wallet agent, for make.fun's `u-phudinhvfx-one-line-slogan-for-a-solana-wallet-agent-a7f6564e` bounty.

The slogan: *"Your wallet minds your SOL and minds the chores."* (48 characters, 9 words)

This slogan sells the wallet agent's real promise in one breath: it looks after your money and it does the work. The double "minds" is the turn — one verb doing two jobs, just like the wallet. "Minds your SOL" is the keeping: your funds held and watched over. "Minds the chores" is the acting: the errands, the signing, the busywork handled without you having to ask. It names the wallet and Solana's SOL up front, so a newcomer knows exactly what it is on one read, with no jargon and no stock crypto line about keys, moons or bags. The parallel structure gives it a rhythm that sticks in the ear and is easy to repeat, and it stays honest: it promises care and chores done, nothing a wallet agent cannot deliver.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the slogan to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically.
